Keyreel Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Keyreel (“the app”) is designed with privacy first. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

What do we collect?

Nothing. Keyreel does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The photos you add, your listing details, your brand details and the videos you create are stored only on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs are used.

Photos

Photos are chosen with the iOS system photo picker, so the app can only access the photos you select. If you save a video to your library, only add-only permission is requested — the app cannot read your library. All video creation happens on your device; no image is uploaded to the internet.

Subscription and free creations

The Keyreel Pro subscription is purchased through the Apple App Store. Keyreel never sees or stores your payment details; the transaction is handled entirely by Apple. Whether your subscription is active is read on your device from the signed transaction record Apple provides, and this information is not sent to us. The number of free creations you have used is kept in your device’s secure storage (Keychain) and never leaves your device.

Notifications

If you use the content calendar, reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device. No server is involved, and notification permission is only requested if you use this feature.

The videos you create

Videos are created on your device and only leave it when you choose to share them. We never receive a copy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page.
