What Girder is — and is not

Girder helps you price a job and record what it actually cost. It is a calculator and a record, not professional advice. The numbers it produces are only as good as the figures you enter and the assumptions you accept. You are responsible for every price you quote and every commitment you make to a customer.

Weights are calculated from section dimensions rather than looked up in a mill table. Hollow sections are computed with sharp corners and run roughly 2% heavy; angles include a root-fillet correction and land within about 0.3% of published tables. These are estimates for quoting, not engineering values. Do not use Girder for structural design, load calculation, or code compliance.
