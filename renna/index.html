Renna — Support

Track an irregular cycle without an app pretending it knows when your period is due. Symptoms beyond weight, your bloodwork over time, and a one-page report for your appointment. Private, on your phone, with no account.

Why Renna sometimes refuses to predict

Most cycle apps are built on a 28-day assumption and always show a single predicted date. When cycles are irregular, that date is a guess dressed up as certainty.

Renna shows a range when your logged cycles support one, and tells you plainly when they don't — for example, "across 4 cycles yours have run 29 to 61 days." It also won't show a fertile window unless you have logged evidence of ovulation (a basal temperature shift or a positive LH test). Saying "we don't know" is the feature, not a gap.

Where the numbers in the app come from

Renna does not invent medical information. The reference ranges, descriptive terms and calculations it shows are published by professional societies and national health institutes:

  • Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) — American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists
  • Polycystic Ovary Syndrome — NICHD, National Institutes of Health
  • Polycystic Ovary Syndrome — Office on Women's Health, U.S. Department of Health and Human Services
  • Polycystic Ovarian Syndrome — StatPearls, National Library of Medicine
  • Insulin Resistance & Prediabetes — NIDDK, National Institutes of Health
  • Metformin — MedlinePlus, U.S. National Library of Medicine
  • Spironolactone — MedlinePlus, U.S. National Library of Medicine

In the app you'll find these under Settings → Where these numbers come from.

Common questions

My cycles are very irregular — is this app still useful?
That is what it is built for. The more irregular your cycles, the more Renna's honesty about ranges beats a fake prediction, and the more useful the symptom analysis becomes.

I don't need to lose weight. Is this app about that?
No. Weight is one entry in a list of eleven symptoms, not the axis everything hangs off.

Where is my data?
Only on your phone. There is no account and no server. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
