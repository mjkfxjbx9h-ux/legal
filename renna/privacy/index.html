Renna Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Renna ("the app") is built privacy-first. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Renna does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Every record you create — your periods, cycle history, symptoms, laboratory results and medications — is stored only on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server. We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Why that matters for cycle data

Menstrual and reproductive health records are among the most sensitive information a person keeps. Because Renna has no server, there is no database of users to breach, subpoena, sell, or hand over — not to us, not to anyone. We cannot produce your data on request because we never have it. That is a deliberate design choice, not a feature we might change later.

Purchases

Renna Pro is offered as an auto-renewing subscription and as a one-time "Lifetime" purchase. All purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. Renna never sees or stores your payment information; the entire transaction is handled by Apple. We receive no identifying information about you from Apple.

Your data, exported by you

Pro features let you generate a PDF report for a clinician or export your records as CSV. These files are created on your device and leave it only when you choose to share them. We never receive a copy.

Deleting your data

All data lives on your device, so deleting the app permanently and completely removes it. You can also erase everything from inside the app at any time under Settings → Delete all data. Because nothing is backed up to a server, deletion is final and cannot be undone by us.

Children's privacy

Renna is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
