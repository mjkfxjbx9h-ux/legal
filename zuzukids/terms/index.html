What ZuzuKids is — and is not

ZuzuKids teaches early reading and writing alongside colours, shapes, numbers and animals, in eleven languages, each with its own alphabet and its own teaching order. It is a practice tool, not a school and not an assessment. It does not diagnose or measure a learning difficulty, and progress inside the app is not a measure of a child’s ability. If you have concerns about a child’s development, speak to a teacher or a health professional.

Lessons are narrated by the device’s own speech synthesiser. Pronunciation is generally good and occasionally imperfect, particularly for individual letter sounds. Treat it as a guide alongside an adult’s voice, not as a replacement for one.

Supervision

The app is designed to be safe to hand to a young child: no advertising, no chat, no links a child can follow out of the app, and a parent gate in front of purchases and settings. It is not a substitute for supervision, and you remain responsible for how and for how long your child uses the device.

The parent PIN

You choose the PIN on first launch. Choose something your child does not know — not their birthday, not 1234. If you forget it, you can reset it from the gate using the device passcode or biometrics; that reset clears the PIN only and leaves profiles and progress untouched. Keep the device passcode itself away from the child, or the reset becomes a way around the gate.
