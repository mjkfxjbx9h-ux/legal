Where our medical information comes from

Thyrowise does not invent medical information. Every reference range, retest interval and medication rule in the app is published by a professional society or a national health institute, and the app links to the original:

  • Thyroid Function Tests — American Thyroid Association
  • Hypothyroidism — American Thyroid Association
  • Hashimoto's Thyroiditis — American Thyroid Association
  • ATA Professional Guidelines — American Thyroid Association
  • Hypothyroidism (Underactive Thyroid) — NIDDK, National Institutes of Health
  • Hashimoto's Disease — NIDDK, National Institutes of Health
  • Levothyroxine — MedlinePlus, U.S. National Library of Medicine

In the app you'll find these under Settings → Medical sources, and next to each statement as a Source badge.

Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

Thyrowise is a tracking and record-keeping tool, not a medical device, and does not provide medical advice or a diagnosis. Reference ranges differ between laboratories — always use the range on your own lab report and the target your doctor set for you.
