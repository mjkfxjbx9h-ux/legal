Lipowise — Support

A record of conservative therapy for lipedema and lymphedema: compression and movement, a 0–10 pain score, circumference measurements, compression garment intervals and dated photographs — exported as one PDF when you need it.

Frequently asked

Where is my data? On your iPhone only. There is no account and no server, and photographs are stored inside the app rather than in your photo library.

Can you recover my record if I lose my phone? No — and that is the trade-off of the design. Nobody but you ever has a copy. Export the PDF regularly and keep your device backup on.

Does it tell me whether I qualify for surgery? No. Lipowise records and formats what you enter; it makes no assessment. Your doctor decides the therapy and your insurer decides the claim.

I am not applying for anything. Switch to “Ongoing care” in More — the countdown disappears and you keep the trends, the garment replacement intervals and the appointment summary.
